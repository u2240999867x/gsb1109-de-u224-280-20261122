Added `attrs.resolve_types(..., in_place=False)` which returns an independent, resolved field view instead of mutating the class's fields.
Each field in the view is resolved against the namespace of the class that actually declared it, so inherited fields keep the meaning their annotation had at its declaration site.
*globalns* and *localns* may be mappings of declaring class to namespace, and *include_extras* is honored per view.
Views are usable from within a ``field_transformer`` and never affect the class or other views, even if resolution fails.
